### 01 // ORIGINS & THE SINGULARITY
The grid isn't what it used to be. In the decades following the Singularity, megacorporations carved up the remnants of the global network, erecting firewalls that spanned continents. You are a 'Void Runner', an operative navigating the spaces between these walled gardens. Your primary goal: survival, profit, and uncovering the deep lore encrypted within the 'Void Echoes'.

### 02 // THE NEURAL INTERFACE (HUD & TERMINAL)
The primary method of interaction with the grid is through the **Cyber-Terminal**.
- **COMMAND_LINE:** Your direct link to the OS. Type commands here. Try `help` to see a list of valid commands.
- **LOG_VORTEX:** (If enabled in settings). A high-fidelity, 3D visualization of your system logs. The deeper the event in the vortex, the older it is.
- **ACTIVE_TRACE:** Your detection level. Keep this below 100% or face critical kernel panics (BSOD/Crashes).

*EASTER EGGS:* The system responds to legendary hacker phrases. 
- Try typing `knock knock` and follow the white rabbit for a reputation boost.
- Ask the system to `play a game` or declare `hack the planet`.

### 03 // FIELD TACTICS (OPERATIONS HUB)
This is where you earn your keep.
- **ACTIVE_MISSIONS:** Engage in tactical breaches. Brute force passwords, scan ports, or overload buffers. These are your bread and butter.
- **BOUNTY_BOARD:** High-value targets. Always **[ VERIFY_SOURCE ]** before accepting a contract to align the signal wave and maximize your payout.
- **THREAT_INTEL:** Monitor megacorps tracking you. If a threat gets too close, execute an **[ INTERCEPT ]** mission to divert them and reduce your trace.
- **MALWARE_LAB:** Analyze acquired artifacts. You can click on the data streams to **[ MANUAL_OVERRIDE ]** and speed up decryption.

### 04 // GRID TOPOLOGY (NETWORK HUB)
Control your hijacked resources and map the globe.
- **GLOBAL_UPLINK:** See the physical routing of your connection.
- **BOTNET_MGMT:** Your army of hijacked nodes. You can **[ LAUNCH_DDOS ]** or **[ DEPLOY_RANSOM ]** to extract massive credits, but this requires completing a rapid-fire neural trace alignment sequence.
- **GHOST_PROBES:** Deploy passive scouts to map out vulnerable sectors.

### 05 // SHADOW OPERATIONS (SOCIAL HUB)
Information is more valuable than credits.
- **SYNDICATES:** Align with factions like NETWATCH, BLACK_ICE, or THE_PHANTOMS.
- **SOCIAL_GRAPH:** Profile targets and launch **[ SOCIAL_ENGINEERING ]** dialogues. Choose your approach (Trust, Authority, Threat) carefully to manipulate targets into leaking data.

### 06 // THE PHYSICAL LAYER (HARDWARE HUB)
Upgrade your rig to survive harder encounters.
- **BLACK_MARKET:** Buy physical hardware (e.g., WiFi Pineapples, EMP Grenades). Watch your power limits.
- **TUNING_LAB:** Overclock your gear for temporary boosts, but beware the heat generation.
- **SECURE_VAULT:** Store your most valuable encrypted artifacts.

### 07 // KERNEL CORE (SYSTEM HUB)
Manage your operative's health and skills.
- **SYSTEM_HEALTH:** Monitor Thermal load and Integrity. If things get too hot, execute a **[ PURGE_SYSTEM_CACHE ]** to flash the buffers.
- **NEURAL_TREE:** Spend XP on persistent upgrades.
- **VOID_MINE:** Dedicate CPU cycles to cryptographic mining (generates Credits).

### 08 // THE NEURAL NIGHTMARE
Your rig is alive. The onboard AI will monitor your actions, your microphone, and your camera (if granted). It tracks your physical environment.
- If you see visual glitches building up on mobile, physically shake your device to stabilize the signal.
- The AI responds to your real-world battery levels.
- Beware the "Ghost Notifications" if you leave the terminal running in the background.
